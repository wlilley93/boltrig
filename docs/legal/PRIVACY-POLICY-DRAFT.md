## What Boltrig is

Boltrig is an invite-only assistant. You sign in to a Boltrig instance, connect an AI provider of
your choice, and ask Boltrig to research, organise and carry out work. Replies are generated by
the AI provider you connect, not by a person. Where your settings require it, Boltrig asks you to
approve an action before it is taken.

## What we collect

- Account details: your email address, the name you give, your role and workspace membership,
  your sign-in sessions and the access tokens issued to your phone or computer.
- Your content: the messages you send and the replies you receive, files and photos you attach
  (up to 25 MiB each), approvals you give or refuse, and the settings you choose.
- Provider keys: the key for the AI provider you connect. It is sealed at rest and never shown
  again after you enter it.
- Records of activity: an audit ledger of actions taken on your behalf, which cannot be edited
  after the fact, and security logs (sign-in attempts, rate-limit events).
- Optional sensing: if you turn on camera or microphone features, the audio or images they
  capture are processed to provide that feature and are not kept beyond it. [Review: confirm the
  exact retention for each sensing path before publication.]
- Device details: the computers you link with Boltrig Desktop (a label and when each was last
  seen).

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other companies' apps or
websites, and the iPhone app collects no analytics.

## Why we process it and on what basis

- To run the service for you: contract.
- To keep the service secure (sign-in records, rate limits, the audit ledger): our legitimate
  interest in security and accountability, and yours.
- Anything optional (for example sensing features): your consent, which you can withdraw by
  turning the feature off.

## Where your data goes

- The AI provider you connect: your messages, attachments that are text, and the context Boltrig
  assembles for a reply are sent to that provider under its own terms. You choose the provider and
  therefore where that data travels; the catalogue includes providers outside the United Kingdom,
  including in countries without a UK adequacy decision.
- Our hosting: servers operated by Hetzner in Germany and Finland.
- Cloudflare: DNS, transport security and connection handling at their edge.
- Transactional email: [provider] for password reset and sign-in emails, when delivery is
  configured.
- Channels you connect (for example Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram, email): the messages you route
  through them.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for access to your data, correction, erasure,
portability, and to object to or restrict processing. Write to [privacy mailbox]. Today you can
delete individual conversations and memories from within Boltrig; [Review: account deletion and a

## Security

Provider keys and other secrets are encrypted at rest; connections use TLS; sign-in supports
two-step verification; actions taken on your behalf are recorded in a ledger that cannot be
altered. Report a security concern to [security mailbox]; see [security page].

## Children

Boltrig is not directed at children and is available by invitation only.
